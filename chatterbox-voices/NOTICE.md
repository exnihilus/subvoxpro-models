licensed under **CC BY 4.0** — <https://www.openslr.org/141/>. LibriTTS-R is derived from LibriTTS
(Zen et al., 2019), itself built from LibriVox public-domain audiobook recordings.

Changes: utterances trimmed, joined with short pauses, loudness-normalised; renamed.
